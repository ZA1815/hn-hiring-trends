Method

  • Source. Threads posted by the whoishiring account, fetched from the public Algolia Hacker News API. Each top-level comment counts as one job post.
  • Classification. A tested pattern library tags each post with technologies, work arrangement, salary ranges, and visa sponsorship. Ambiguous words such as “Go”, “React” and “Node” are matched case-sensitively and with exclusions (“go-to”, “react to”).
  • Salaries. USD annual figures only (“$140k–$180k”, “$165,000”). Hourly, monthly, funding and bonus amounts are excluded. Months with fewer than 15 salaried posts are left out of the median.
  • Work arrangement. Posts mentioning remote and on-site, or hybrid, count as hybrid. Before mid-2015 the thread format did not ask for location, so many early posts are “unspecified”.
  • Updates. A scheduled GitHub Actions job refreshes the latest threads daily and redeploys this page. The current month appears once its thread closes.
